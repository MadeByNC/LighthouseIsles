Lighthouse Isles – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21.10.2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Lighthouse Isles ("the game") handles, why, and what choices you have. The game is made by Encek Games ("we", "us").

In short: we do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, e-mail address or other personal details ourselves. The game uses services from Google (Google Play Games Services and Google AdMob), which process some data as described below.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress (islands, resources, upgrades, statistics) and your settings (volume, language, vibration) are saved in the game's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you use cloud saves (section 2). Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Google Play Games Services

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for:

  • Sign-in – your Play Games player ID, display name and profile picture are provided by Google. We use them only to show leaderboards inside the game.
  • Leaderboards – your scores (flag record, total fame, fastest lighthouse time) are sent to Google and shown to other players together with your Play Games display name and profile picture, according to your Play Games profile visibility settings.
  • Cloud saves – a copy of your game progress is stored in your Google account (Saved Games), so you can continue on another device.

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in to Play Games; leaderboards and cloud saves are then unavailable. You can delete Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, or the per-game data option).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads: an ad is shown only when you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. To show ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about ad interactions, app usage and diagnostics (e.g. crashes, performance).

This data is used for showing ads, measuring them, analytics and preventing fraud. It is processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): when required, the game asks for your consent using Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can refuse; you may then see non-personalised ads. You can reset or limit ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run our own servers or analytics, and we do not receive your e-mail address or contacts.
  • The game has no chat and no in-game messaging between players.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Security and retention

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your device stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's policies and your Google account settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to or restrict processing. Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled through your Google account and Play Games settings. You can also contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the game gets new features. The date at the top shows the latest version.
